# Gap analysis evaluation (synthetic data)

Generated by `python -m sim.evaluate`. These are **made-up companies where we know the truth**,
so this shows whether the method works and how to set it, not how it performs on real company data.

## Set-up

- Each row: 60 companies, each with 6 managers x 6 employees and 2 clients per manager.
- 15% of manager-employee pairs are biased (half rated up, half rated down), unless the row says otherwise.
- Rating noise: 0.55 stars for managers, 0.55 for clients. Each manager and client is randomly a little strict or generous. Ratings are rounded to 1-5 stars.
- FairView settings: |z| >= 2.5, at least 10 rated pieces of work for the pair (or all of them when the row has fewer), at least 10 for the manager's other employees.
- Random seed 2026, so the numbers are the same on every run.

**Precision**: of the pairs flagged, the share that were really biased. **Recall**: of the biased pairs, the share that were flagged. **False alarms**: of the fair pairs, the share flagged anyway. **AUC**: how well |z| ranks biased pairs above fair ones (0.5 = coin flip, 1.0 = perfect).
